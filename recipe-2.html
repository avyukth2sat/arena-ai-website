Method

Swaps are highlighted right inside the steps.

  1. 1

    Marinate the chicken with the yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, garam masala with extra cinnamon and cloves, sweet paprika with a pinch of cayenne, slit serrano peppers, half the crispy fried onions, half the mint and cilantro, lemon juice, 2 tbsp ghee and salt for at least 2 hours.

  2. 2

    Soak the rice for 30 minutes. Warm the milk and steep the saffron in it.

  3. 3

    Boil a big pot of heavily salted water with the whole spices, cumin seeds with a pinch of caraway, green cardamom pods with a pinch of smoked paprika and ground nutmeg. Par-cook the rice for 5–6 minutes until about 70% done; drain.

  4. 4

    Spread the chicken in a heavy pot and layer the rice on top, then drizzle with the saffron milk, rose water, the remaining ghee, crispy fried onions and herbs.

  5. 5

    Seal the pot tightly with foil and the lid (dum). Cook on high for 5 minutes, then on the lowest heat for 40 minutes.

  6. 6

    Rest 10 minutes, then gently mix the layers from the side as you serve with raita.

Kitchen notes

  • 💡The rice must be only 70% cooked before layering — it finishes in the steam.
  • 💡Set a flat griddle under the pot during dum to prevent scorching.
  • 💡Taste as you go — substitutes can be milder or saltier than the originals, so adjust seasoning at the end.
