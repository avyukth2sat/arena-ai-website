Method

Swaps are highlighted right inside the steps.

  1. 1

    Blend the tomatoes, red bell peppers, 1 onion and habanero peppers until smooth. Simmer the purée for 10–15 minutes until reduced by about a third.

  2. 2

    Wash the rice in several changes of water until it runs clear, then drain well.

  3. 3

    Heat the oil in a heavy pot and fry the second onion (sliced) until soft. Stir in the tomato paste and fry 3–4 minutes until it darkens to a deep brick red.

  4. 4

    Add the reduced pepper purée, mild curry powder with extra turmeric, dried thyme, bay leaves and crumbled Maggi cubes. Fry, stirring, until the oil floats to the top — about 10 minutes.

  5. 5

    Stir in the rice and stock, season with salt, then cover tightly with foil and the lid. Cook on low heat for 25–30 minutes without stirring.

  6. 6

    When the rice is tender, raise the heat for 2–3 minutes to build the smoky 'party' bottom, then fold in the butter and serve with fried plantains.

Kitchen notes

  • 💡Party jollof gets its smoky flavor from slightly scorching the bottom — don't skip that last high-heat minute.
  • 💡Parboiled rice (like Ben's Original) absorbs the stew without turning mushy.
  • 💡Taste as you go — substitutes can be milder or saltier than the originals, so adjust seasoning at the end.
