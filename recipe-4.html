Method

Swaps are highlighted right inside the steps.

  1. 1

    Cut the pork into bite-size pieces and the kimchi into strips, saving any juice.

  2. 2

    Sauté the pork in sesame oil until it renders and browns, then add the kimchi, garlic and sugar. Stir-fry 5 minutes until the kimchi softens.

  3. 3

    Stir in the Aleppo pepper and gochujang, then pour in the chicken broth with a splash of fish sauce and the kimchi juice.

  4. 4

    Add the sliced onion, bring to a boil and simmer 15 minutes.

  5. 5

    Slide in thick slices of tofu and simmer 5 minutes more. Top with scallions and serve bubbling with sushi rice.

Kitchen notes

  • 💡Older, sourer kimchi makes the best jjigae.
  • 💡A slice of American cheese melted on top is a beloved Korean twist.
  • 💡Taste as you go — substitutes can be milder or saltier than the originals, so adjust seasoning at the end.
