Produce

Scotch bonnet peppers

🇳🇬 🇬🇭 🇯🇲

Nigerian · Ghanaian · Jamaican · Trinidadian

🧭 The real thing: Latin & Caribbean markets (Fiesta, Sedano's, local tiendas) or African & Caribbean markets

  • Habanero peppers

    92%

    How much: 1:1 — same count

    Habaneros are the same species (Capsicum chinense) with nearly identical fruity heat; scotch bonnets are just a touch sweeter.

    📍 Any supermarket

  • Serrano peppers + pinch of cayenne

    66%

    How much: 2 serranos + ⅛ tsp cayenne per bonnet

    Matches the heat level but misses the tropical, apricot-like aroma.

    📍 Any supermarket

Pantry

Red palm oil

🇳🇬 🇬🇭 🇧🇷

Nigerian · Ghanaian · Brazilian

🧭 The real thing: African & Caribbean markets or natural grocers (Whole Foods, Sprouts)

  • Annatto-infused vegetable oil

    80%

    How much: Warm ½ cup oil with 1 tbsp annatto seeds for 5 min, then strain

    Annatto gives the same brick-red color plus an earthy, peppery note close to palm oil.

    📍 Hispanic aisle · International aisle

  • Vegetable oil + sweet paprika + turmeric

    68%

    How much: ½ cup oil + 1 tsp paprika + ¼ tsp turmeric

    Nails the color; the flavor is milder, so lean on stock and bouillon.

    📍 Any supermarket

    How much: 1:1 by volume

    Rich and nutty; slightly more bitter, so toast lightly first.

    📍 Any supermarket
