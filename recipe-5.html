Method

Swaps are highlighted right inside the steps.

  1. 1

    Season the meat with half the chopped onion, one of the Maggi cubes and salt. Simmer with the stockfish in 4 cups of water until tender, about 40 minutes. Reserve the stock.

  2. 2

    Mix the ground egusi with a few tablespoons of water into a thick paste.

  3. 3

    Warm the red palm oil in a large pot over medium heat (don't let it smoke). Fry the remaining onion, then spoon in the egusi paste in clumps and fry 5–7 minutes, turning, until it curdles and turns golden.

  4. 4

    Gradually pour in the meat stock, then add the meat, ground crayfish, iru, blended scotch bonnet peppers and the remaining Maggi cubes. Simmer 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.

  5. 5

    Stir in the ugu leaves and cook 3–5 minutes until just wilted. Adjust the salt.

  6. 6

    Serve hot with pounded yam, eba or rice.

Kitchen notes

  • 💡Fry the egusi in clumps without over-stirring — the curds are the best part.
  • 💡Egusi freezes beautifully and tastes even better reheated.
  • 💡Good news: Fiesta Mart should carry authentic ground egusi (melon seeds), red palm oil, stockfish (dried cod) — worth the extra stop.
