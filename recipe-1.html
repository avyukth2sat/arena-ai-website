Method

Swaps are highlighted right inside the steps.

  1. 1

    Parboil the bones for 10 minutes, then drain and rinse them — the secret to a crystal-clear broth.

  2. 2

    Char the halved onions and ginger directly over a flame or under the broiler until blackened in spots.

  3. 3

    Toast the star anise, cinnamon, cloves, coriander seeds and green cardamom pods with a pinch of smoked paprika in a dry pan until fragrant, then tie them in a spice bag.

  4. 4

    Return the bones to a clean pot with 5 quarts of water, the brisket, charred aromatics and turbinado sugar. Simmer very gently for 3 hours, skimming often; pull the brisket at 1½ hours and add the spice bag for the final hour.

  5. 5

    Season the strained broth with fish sauce and salt. Prepare the dried flat rice noodles according to the package and slice the eye of round paper-thin.

  6. 6

    Divide the noodles, sliced brisket and raw beef among bowls and ladle the boiling broth over to cook the beef. Serve with Italian basil with a few mint leaves, extra cilantro (stems included), bean sprouts, lime and Thai chilies.

Kitchen notes

  • 💡Freeze the eye of round for 20 minutes so it slices paper-thin.
  • 💡Never boil the broth hard — a lazy simmer keeps it clear.
  • 💡Taste as you go — substitutes can be milder or saltier than the originals, so adjust seasoning at the end.
