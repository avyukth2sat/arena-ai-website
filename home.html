How it works

Your home kitchen, translated for American aisles.

Most diaspora cooks know the feeling: the recipe is in your heart, but half the ingredients are an ocean away. Culturize closes that gap with a flavor knowledge base built from how families actually cook abroad.

  1. 🍲

    STEP 01

    Tell us the dish

    Pick from our kitchen library — jollof, phở, mole, mansaf and more — or paste your family's handwritten recipe.

  2. 🛒

    STEP 02

    We scan stores near you

    From your ZIP we map nearby supermarkets and international markets, and what each one typically stocks.

  3. ✨

    STEP 03

    Cook with smart swaps

    Get the recipe rewritten around flavor-matched substitutes, with a store-by-store list, aisles and prices.
